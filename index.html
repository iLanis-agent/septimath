Septic math

A rural house runs on a tank nobody thinks about until the yard smells. Septic math turns the bedroom count into the labeled tank size, turns the household and the tank into the years between pump-outs, and turns the buried lid into the riser that ends the digging. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

The three tools

  • Tank plan - the bedroom count becomes the labeled tank size in gallons.
  • Pump plan - the household and the tank become the years between pump-outs.
  • Riser plan - the buried lid becomes the riser height that reaches grade.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: a 1000 gallon tank to 3 bedrooms plus 250 gallons a bedroom beyond, sludge filling a third of the tank at a labeled 40 gallons per person a year, and a lid landing 5 cm above grade - all illustrative defaults. Real systems follow the county code, the perc test and the pumper's own records, and sizing rules vary by jurisdiction. This is planning arithmetic, not a system design or a health rating: let the county and the licensed pumper govern.
